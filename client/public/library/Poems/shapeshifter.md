---
title: shapeshifter
date: 2026-07
---
I’m no stranger to the engine roar

That scores my bolting thoughts

My hands wringing in my lap

Too damp to grip my seat

I can only stare ahead

The scenery, distant and blear

It pulls me into a trance

And when it floods my peripheral,

I’ve crossed the line of no return.

I befriend the flora and fauna

The solar might, the crops’ order

How people eat and work and play

I project my life into their’s

A shapeshifter stumbling upon kin

I am one with this territory

I am a citizen of this town

The steering wheel jolts away

Forsaking my precious town!

The scenery morphs into another

My vision blurs, my sight shifting

Into another distant and blear view


I find myself alien again

The whirling engine deafening my thoughts,

I can only stare ahead.
