this fleeting sense of unfamiliarity

oh, how i despise

it seems to dismantle my sanity

sounds of a bittersweet reprise

a creature of habit taken abroad,

the air of uncertainty chokes me

the act of falling apart, it haunts me

i’ve never felt so feeble, so distraught

a weed growing into the spiral of my thought

i’ve taken daydreams and molded them into reality

cogs in my brain turning night and day

though it’s done nothing but distract me

for i live still, in constant dismay

what can i say,

maybe chasing one’s dreams wasn’t the way at the end of the day.
