I have nothing to hold on to,  

Nothing but a binary trail  

Erase these ones and zeros  

And it’s no different from a telltale  

  

I’m often dragged in train of thought  

One I did not consent to endure  

What voice do I have in your head?  

Did you ever plan to hear it all?  

  

Those helpless inquiries turned reality  

It really couldn’t get any stranger  

You used to speak in prose and poetry  

The words hastily deemed too major  

  

I spent years convincing that kid  

It was something more than counterfeit  

But it was cruel, and it was cheap  

And it shouldn’t mean a thing to me  

  

This is the trail's terminal, I promise  

You’ll be absent from my future words  

So long, the fireflies and valley bars  

For they thrive at replacing old scars  

  

Sure, I’ll grieve a few Septembers  

But the other months will make up for it  

And soon this fable will vanish within  

The vast binary sea we abide in.
