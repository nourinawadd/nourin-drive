i was just starting to get better,

i saw the distant lights ahead of me

i basked in its glory

and cherished its amenity.

but as the story goes,

there was a storm coming my way

but blinded by the secluded lights,

i didn’t get to slip away.

didn’t take too long for it hit me,

i was robbed of what i found

i got shoved, fell head-first and

descended into the underground.

as i lowered in altitude,

i felt myself slipping away

with parts of who i am

getting stuck on the way

as i reached the bottom,

i saw the outline of a bruised stranger

it felt like looking into a mirror

a parallel, one endangered.

as i pondered on the chances

of worlds clashing into a instant,

your hand reached out to me,

with a smile so distant.

though my heart gushed with hope

with unease it was intertwined,

for i knew deep down

this was trouble by design.
