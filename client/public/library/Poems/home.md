_karibu nyumbani_

i built myself a simple home

with a normal bed and regular sink

stained my ordinary oak table black

by the drops of this quill filled with ink

i like keeping my blinds up

it opens up the space a bit

as i head to the kitchen, it helps

seeing my coffee table sunlit

walking through the hallway, i

can’t help but notice the desolate air

you could say it’s too wide

too much room for echoes inside

i could hear the frequencies collide

the tone of my voice resides

inside these walls, they’re too high

they amplify the taste of cyanide

in my words, they echo right back

to my ear, i’m terrified

but that’s besides the point,

right?

i discern the possibilities endless

as i sit at my kitchen table

i could write my magnum opus

or i could pick on this stupid sticker label

i’ll sip on my morning coffee

as i reflect on the reality i’m given

it may only be the crack of dawn

but wouldn’t you like to know how i’m living?

life is suffering, that i know

but perhaps i bear wreckage of my own

the everlasting torment one undergoes,

could it be the libretto of their own show?

i know i'm not sane in the head

but i live a typical life, i think

inside the simple home i’ve built

with a normal bed and a regular sink.
