a poem i wrote on my flight

/

i’m stuck on this layover flight

that’s had me between states of being

what i’ve made of what i was given

and what’s left of this life to be

i choose the default options

i give into a written path

i ride into what’s expected of me

but do i fathom the aftermath?

and you, you have me sat on a pedestal

higher than i was ever able to reach

i strived for your shiny, polished ideals

yet it was nothing but a figure of speech

and i’ve spent sleepless nights tucked away

for your eyes were nothing but judgemental

wondering if i’ll ever live to become

the golden frame of my potential

you’ve poured a drink on my wiring

short a fuse, all cylinders firing

it’s always been a bloody wreckage

but maybe i won’t take it this time

and maybe the stars are not in my reach

and maybe i’ll never be more than ordinary

and mabye, just maybe, if i believed this

i could ease the knot i tied myself

and i’ll be just fine on the layover flight

knowing there’s a certain destination.
