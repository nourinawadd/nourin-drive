i'm beginning to understand

there’s no mystery to who i am

it's the simplest things I need

just a friend and a place to sleep

and as my borrowed time drags out,

a force pulls my feet to the ground

the once unsettling presence of my self

now exists a familiar book on a shelf

the shadows once making my skin crawl

are now only a distant memory i recall

and this foreign life left me forlorn

but now comforts my haggard soul

on the grounds of Fintas, i now lie

as i wave this land a bittersweet goodbye

winds of the past gushes with implore

but this place isn’t home anymore

i’m glad i made a sacrifice

albeit clueless at the time

it’s led me to this still image

lined with hope too bright to diminish

i saw it through an open door

an older me i’ve met before

although my yesterday would deter,

i’m finally elated to find her
