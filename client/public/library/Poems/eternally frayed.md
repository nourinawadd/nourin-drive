a simple tape looping evermore,

my days couldn’t get less exciting

tried adding thrill but what for,

it’s not like it’ll rile the wave i’m riding

though i can’t help but ponder

on the verity of eternal existence

our souls created and never to be destroyed,

without a fidget of our inference

sometimes, this gives me relief

since every circumstance is but brief

all the late nights i spend confining my grief

trying to stay quiet, set to avert familial heed

i remind myself of the better times i have foreseen

and alleviate the endurance to live the glee i have guaranteed

sometimes, this gives me gloom

with the overwhelming chase of life

with every competition fazed alike

expectations through no wrongs and every right

it drains me, this rope of apathy pulling tight

and its right here that eternity doesn’t seem so bright

don’t get me wrong, i’m not wishing for an optional grave

but though this aeon may hold some greats,

i just wish i was never made,

for i am eternally frayed.
