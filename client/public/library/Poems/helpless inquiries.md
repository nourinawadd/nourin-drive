the thought of the door kept ajar never leaves my mind

it lets lethal questions slither through the cracks

what if i pushed the concrete door shut myself

before it turns into a pile of feathers before my eyes

and the wind of time gushes to blow it away?

the thought of giving up my saving grace terrifies me

yet what was once comfortable leaves me uneasy

that fear hung in the air, what if it’s dissolving what’s left of this?

how can i expect it to live with nothing to feed on?

patience was never my strong suit,

but now i'm only holding onto it for dear life

and for dear life, i hope to give it my all.
